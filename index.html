Caelan Ritter

My name is Caelan Ritter, and I am a postdoctoral scholar in mathematics at Vanderbilt University. I received my Ph.D. in 2026 at the University of Washington under Farbod Shokrieh. I am interested broadly in algebraic and tropical geometry, with a focus on the intersection theory of tropical abelian varieties.

You can reach me by email at caelan.ritter@vanderbilt.edu; I have an OpenPGP key.
